# Microsoft Corporation (MSFT)

*Equity research note -- as of 2026-09-06*

## Business Summary

Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) is a Technology company with trailing twelve-month revenue of $331.8B and a net margin of +40.3%. Shares last traded at $499.70, a trailing P/E of 27.9x.

## Catalyst / Thesis

Microsoft's next quarterly earnings report is the near-term catalyst, specifically Azure growth guidance set against its capital expenditure trajectory. The trailing free cash flow figure used in this note ($16.5B) is unusually low relative to Microsoft's $182.9B in trailing operating cash flow, reflecting a period of exceptionally heavy capex as the company builds out AI data center capacity. The thesis question this catalyst answers is whether that capex is converting into Azure AI revenue fast enough to justify the near-term cash flow compression, or whether the buildout is outrunning realized demand. A 15% FCF growth assumption over the explicit period reflects a base case where that capex begins paying back; a materially lower growth rate would push the DCF estimate even further below the figures below.

## Key Risks

- AI infrastructure capex continues outpacing realized Azure AI revenue, prolonging free cash flow compression.
- Price and infrastructure competition from AWS and Google Cloud pressures Azure margins.
- Regulatory or antitrust scrutiny of Microsoft's software bundling practices.
- The $16.5B trailing free cash flow figure used here is likely depressed by near-term capex rather than representative of normalized cash-generating power; using it directly in the DCF is a stated conservative simplification, not a claim that this is Microsoft's steady-state cash flow.
