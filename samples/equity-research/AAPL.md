# Apple Inc. (AAPL)

*Equity research note -- as of 2026-09-06*

## Business Summary

Apple Inc. (AAPL) is a Technology company with trailing twelve-month revenue of $466.8B and a net margin of +27.6%. Shares last traded at $319.97, a trailing P/E of 36.6x.

## Catalyst / Thesis

Apple's next quarterly earnings call is the near-term catalyst: the market is watching whether double-digit Services segment growth and the AI feature rollout across the iPhone lineup can sustain the premium multiple the stock trades at today. The DCF and comparable-multiples estimates below both fall well short of the current price, which is a common pattern for a mega-cap compounder priced on its growth trajectory rather than trailing cash flow: a DCF anchored to today's free cash flow structurally understates a business the market expects to keep compounding. The real question this catalyst answers is whether forward guidance shows installed-base monetization (Services, wearables) growing fast enough to justify that gap, or whether iPhone unit growth is decelerating faster than the current multiple assumes.

## Key Risks

- iPhone unit growth continues decelerating in a saturated smartphone replacement cycle.
- Regulatory pressure on App Store commission rates compresses Services segment margin.
- Share loss to local competitors in China, Apple's largest ex-US market.
- This DCF is anchored to trailing free cash flow with a flat growth assumption; it is a conservative floor, not a forecast, and will understate value if Services and AI features accelerate the growth rate above the 7% assumed here.
